### Step 1: Set up Claude Opus 5.5 & Install the Open-Source "Retention" Skill

First, open Claude Opus 5.5 (via Claude Code or your agent terminal) and install the official open-source skill:
👉 **[Retention on GitHub](https://github.com/Andrea13235/Retention)**

Here is what the Retention skill actually does under the hood:
1. **Whisper Transcription Engine:** It ingests your raw video files and transcribes every single spoken word with millisecond-accurate timecodes.
2. **Narrative & Pacing Analysis:** Instead of blindly cutting, the skill analyzes the transcript to detect dead-air silences (>350ms), vocal filler words, storytelling open-loops, and retention drop-off risks.
3. **HyperFrames Render Pipeline:** It connects to the HyperFrames rendering engine to generate dynamic cuts, punch-zooms, kinetic text overlays, and atomic motion graphics without ever touching a heavy video editor.

---

### Step 2: Connect the RetentionVolt MCP Server

Autonomous agents are powerful, but they don't have "taste" or pacing intuition out of the box. 

This is where **RetentionVolt MCP** comes in. 

RetentionVolt is essentially the *Mobbin for video editing and retention intelligence*. By connecting its native MCP server, you feed Opus 5.5 direct access to a curated database of reverse-engineered videos from the top 1% of creators (MrBeast, Vox, Ali Abdaal, Cleo Abram):
• **Exact Cut Cadences:** Feeds Opus 5.5 the 2.18s Average Shot Length (ASL) and multi-cam rotation rules so it knows precisely when to cut.
• **Pattern Interrupts & Motion Graphics:** Injects proven easing formulas and CSS code for Apple-style pop-ups and Vox-style 3D grid sequences.
• **Thumbnail Intelligence Vault:** Automatically suggests high-CTR thumbnail prompts for Midjourney and FLUX based on proven curiosity gaps.

To connect it to your agent, simply add the MCP endpoint:
`https://retentionvolt.com/api/mcp`

---

### Step 3: Drop Your RAW Video & Trigger the Edit with `/`

Now comes the magic part:

1. Drag and drop your raw camera takes (even a single continuous video shot from your iPhone) directly into the chat or terminal.
2. Type the slash command to invoke the skill:
   `"/retention edit my raw footage using high-energy MrBeast pacing"`
   *(or choose cinematic documentary pacing for educational content).*

Opus 5.5 takes over:
• Whisper transcribes and cleans all filler words and breath gaps over 350ms.
• It queries RetentionVolt MCP for the pacing cadence and camera angle switches.
• HyperFrames compiles and renders the final MP4 with smooth kinetic captions, punch-zooms (+18%), and motion design cards.

In under 5 minutes, you get a finished video that looks like it took a $50/hour editor two full days to assemble.
