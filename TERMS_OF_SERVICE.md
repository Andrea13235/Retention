## 4. PLATFORM DESCRIPTION & CORE CAPABILITIES

4.1. RETENTION.BIN is a next-generation **Video Reference Intelligence Platform** (often described as "the Mobbin for video editing and YouTube retention"), providing quantitative and qualitative structural insights into top-performing digital videos.

4.2. **Core Capabilities include**:
* **Video Cadence Deconstructions**: Microsecond-accurate timeline breakdowns measuring Cuts Per Minute (CPM), Average Shot Length (ASL), Words Per Minute (WPM), audio silence dropouts, b-roll insertions, and hook retention curves.
* **Thumbnail Vault & Blueprints**: High-CTR thumbnail compositions reverse-engineered with eye-tracking heatmaps, focal point vectors, and generative prompt breakdowns.
* **Motion Graphics Vault**: Programmatic motion assets, lower thirds, sound design cue markers, and direct code exports (CSS animations, React Remotion components).
* **NLE Timeline Exports**: One-click generation of industry-standard edit decision lists (`.EDL`, `.XML`) for Adobe Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, and Final Cut Pro.
* **AI Video Audit Tool**: Automated rhythm diagnosis of user-submitted video URLs or clips.
* **Model Context Protocol (MCP) Server**: High-throughput JSON-RPC/REST server (`/api/mcp`) enabling direct semantic retrieval by local and cloud AI developer agents (Claude Desktop, Cursor IDE, OpenAI Codex, Windsurf).
