## Team License

You are required to obtain a Team License (paid subscription) to use Andrea
Video Skill if you are not within the group of entities eligible for the
Free License — in practice, for-profit teams of 4 or more people. This
license enables you to use CutCraft for the allowed use cases
specified in the Free License.

To request a Team License, open an issue on the GitHub repository
(https://github.com/Andrea13235/cutcraft/issues) with subject
"Team License request" and describe your team size and use case. Pricing is
agreed upon request, based on team size.
